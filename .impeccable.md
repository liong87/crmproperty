# Design Context — Lanthorn Properties CRM

Recorded from the codebase's own design commentary, `DESIGNSYSTEM.md`, and the
competitor teardown that shaped the current look. Read this before any design work;
it is what stops a "polish pass" from quietly becoming a rebrand.

## Users

Five people in a Malaysian property agency: one principal (admin), one team lead, and
three agents. Agents work leads from a phone between viewings and in sales galleries;
the principal reads reports on a laptop. The job is to work today's leads, keep
appointments, and not lose a client to a colleague's silence. The competing product is
not another CRM — it is WhatsApp. Anything slower than a WhatsApp message loses.

## Brand personality

Warm, grounded, unfussy. Three words: **tropical, plain-spoken, professional.**
Not startup-slick, not enterprise-grey.

## Aesthetic direction — DO NOT DRIFT

- **Palette:** deep teal primary (`--primary` 179 74% 19%), warm amber accent, warm
  off-white canvas (`42 30% 96%`). The tinted canvas is load-bearing: it is what lets
  cards carry a hairline and NO shadow.
- **Type:** Fraunces on `h1`/`.font-display`, Inter for everything else. **Fraunces
  stays.** It is the single most distinctive thing about the app and the teardown was
  explicit about keeping it. This overrides any general advice to avoid it.
- **Shadow budget:** exactly one — the active segmented tab. Spending it anywhere else
  makes the tab stop reading as selected.
- **Radius:** a closed 8/12/16 scale (`rounded-lg` / `rounded-xl` / `rounded-2xl`).
- **One gradient:** `--primary` → `--primary-2` for primary surfaces, plus the single
  off-canvas glow in `.app-shell`. Never green-to-amber; that reads as a warning.
- **Funnel ramp:** `--stage-1..5`, neutral to brand green. Deliberately not amber/red
  mid-funnel — normal drop-off should not look like an alarm.
- **Anti-references:** the competitor's blue, their geometric sans on headings, stat-tile
  grids at the top of every page, decorative sparklines.

## Design principles

1. **Density is the feature.** Rows visible at once beats whitespace. Reduce padding
   before you reduce columns.
2. **One number and a clause**, never a grid of tiles, at the top of a page — the header
   decides what to care about instead of asking the reader to.
3. **Colour is never the only signal.** Every tone carries a word or a glyph.
4. **The keyboard path is the real path.** If it cannot be reached with Tab it does not
   exist; hover-only affordances need a `focus-within` twin.
5. **Confirm the irreversible, not the reversible.** A soft delete asks once; a
   conversion that makes a record read-only asks harder.
